Welcome to Goa!

Goa is one of the most popular tourist destinations in India. It is famous for its beautiful beaches, Portuguese architecture, churches, seafood and nightlife.

Whether you want to relax near the beach or explore historical places, Goa has something for everyone.

About Goa

Destination Information

Goa is located on the western coast of India. It is known for its scenic coastline, beautiful sunsets and cultural heritage.

Best Time to Visit

November to February is the best time to visit Goa because the weather is pleasant.

Popular Attractions

  • Baga Beach
  • Calangute Beach
  • Fort Aguada
  • Basilica of Bom Jesus
  • Dudhsagar Waterfalls

Estimated Budget

Approximately ₹8,000 - ₹15,000 per person for a 3-day trip.

Travel Tips

  • Carry sunscreen and sunglasses.
  • Keep your important documents safe.
  • Book hotels in advance during holidays.
  • Try local Goan food.
  • Follow beach safety rules.
